Burger Calling is a modern 100% pure-vegetarian quick-service burger brand operating across Chhattisgarh, India. Built around bold recipes, crisp hygiene standards, and youthful café aesthetics, we invite entrepreneurs to launch their own Burger Calling outlet across Chhattisgarh.

2. Available Franchise Models

Format A
Express Kiosk

Compact counter or stall for colleges, busy markets, stations, and fuel plazas.

Format B

Format C
Signature Restaurant

Full-scale 100% pure-veg burger café offering family dine-in, takeaway, and delivery.
